DESIGN CONFLICTS  |  KMS DELIVERY REVIEW				
				
Confidentiality trade-offs need explicit feature-mode behavior, consent and product disclosure rather than silent functionality fallback.

CF-01	Zero-Knowledge files vs server OCR/search/AI	Use trusted-client processing; otherwise managed mode after explicit classification approval.	R20 / R34 / R35	Open
CF-02	Zero-Knowledge malware inspection vs opaque ciphertext	Admit reduced server assurance; client scan, device posture, staging/quarantine and policy restrictions.	R19 / R32	Open
CF-03	Strong confidentiality vs forgotten keys	Choose no recovery (permanent loss possible) or independently governed quorum recovery (extra access path).	R20 / R26	Open
CF-04	Authorization revocation vs offline access	Revoke future delivery; clearly disclose inability to erase previously copied plaintext or keys.	R21 / R37	Open
CF-05	Group central oversight vs subsidiary confidentiality	Keep control-plane and document-key permissions separate; aggregated group reporting.	R02 / R21	Open
CF-06	Legal hold vs right to erasure / crypto erasure	Apply jurisdiction-specific priority with recorded legal justification, review and execution gates.	R12 / R13 / R14	Open
CF-07	E2EE vs court-directed eDiscovery	Adopt legal custodian export or trustee recovery procedure; do not imply invisible server decryption.	R13 / R20	Open
CF-08	Browser client E2EE vs compromised code delivery	Use independently hosted signed client and verification; do not trust JavaScript supplied by WordPress.	R20 / R37	Open
CF-09	Enterprise coauthoring vs E2EE	Use locally trusted E2EE editing protocol or classify as managed collaboration; do not silently downgrade.	R30 / R37	Open
CF-10	AI answers vs authorization changes	Re-evaluate permissions per source and at answer time, purge embeddings and caches, constrain outputs.	R35 / R21	Open
CF-11	Audit richness vs privacy and subsidiary secrecy	Redact/minimize payloads; permission-tiered logs; independent integrity and retention.	R27 / R14	Open
CF-12	Digital approval vs legal electronic signature	Separate workflow acknowledgments from vendor-backed signature evidence and jurisdiction-specific requirements.	R13 / R33	Open
CF-13	Third-party editor vs content confidentiality	Vet data handling and prevent plaintext transfer of protected files to unapproved editors.	R09 / R20	Open
CF-14	Public KB publishing vs hidden tenant content	Use approved declassification with separate public projection and no anonymous backdoor queries.	R15 / R19	Open
CF-15	Migration convenience vs verified historical entitlements	Use signed dry-runs, least-privilege ACL mapping, dual approval and auditable exceptions.	R42 / R21	Open
